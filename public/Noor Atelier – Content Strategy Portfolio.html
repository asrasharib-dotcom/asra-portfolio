Content strategy for fine jewellery

A portfolio project: a complete social media plan for Noor Atelier, a fictional handmade silver and gold-plated jewellery label, built around the festive and wedding season.

Worn daily,
kept for years

Strategy and planning

Jewellery is a considered purchase. People need to trust the craft, picture the piece on themselves, and find a reason to buy now. The plan moves followers through those three steps.

Goal

Grow followers by 25% and bring monthly online orders up 30% over the December season.

Audience

Women aged 22 to 38 who buy for themselves, for gifts and for weddings, and who value handmade pieces over mass-market ones.

Voice

Warm, calm and precise. Plain words, short sentences, and no pressure tactics.

Journey

Discover through Reels and Pinterest, trust through craft and reviews, then buy through Stories and WhatsApp.

Content pillars and creative direction

Four pillars keep the feed varied but recognisable. Every post belongs to exactly one.

30% of posts

Craft and process

Show how each piece is made, from sketch to polish.

  • Casting and stone-setting close-ups
  • Maker interviews
  • Materials explained
30% of posts

Styling and wearing

Help people picture each piece in their own life.

  • Layering and stacking guides
  • Outfit pairings
  • One piece, three ways
25% of posts

Gifting and occasions

Give people a reason and a shortlist.

  • Gift guides by budget
  • Bridal and festive edits
  • Packaging and cards
15% of posts

Community and proof

Let customers do the persuading.

  • Reviews and customer photos
  • Polls and Q&A
  • Care tips

Creative direction

Soft Ivory#F5F7F4 · backgrounds
Deep Navy#263847 · text and headlines
Teal Blue#527D89 · focus and accents
Soft Grey Aqua#EAF0EF · cards and panels

Photography: soft daylight, ivory or grey-aqua backdrops, hands and skin tones over mannequins, one piece per frame. Type: a high-contrast serif for headlines and a clean sans-serif for captions.

Platform-specific ideas

Each platform has one job. The same idea is reshaped, not copied.

Instagram

4 feed posts and daily Stories a week
  • Reel: wax-to-ring casting in 30 seconds
  • Carousel: how to layer three necklace lengths
  • Stories: polls, countdowns and order shout-outs
  • Highlights: Care, Gifting, Reviews, Sizing

TikTok

2 videos a week
  • Packing an order, in real time
  • “Things I wish I knew before buying silver”
  • Try-on with three outfits, one earring
  • Behind-the-scenes mistakes and fixes

Pinterest

3 pins a week
  • Bridal jewellery mood boards
  • Gift ideas by budget
  • Stacking and layering inspiration
  • Each pin links to a product page

Facebook and WhatsApp

2 posts a week plus a broadcast
  • Customer reviews and unboxing photos
  • Weekly new-arrival broadcast
  • Order updates and sizing help
  • Early access for returning customers
